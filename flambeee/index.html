Play a game

Nine games and six apps, free in the browser. Nothing to install, no account, no ads.

The whole arcade

Nine games

  • Wordfire. One word a day; guess the 5-letter word and keep your streak.
  • Cinder. A BBS-style text RPG with a new quest every UTC day.
  • Afterglow. Lights the board, goes dark, and takes moves from memory.
  • Echo Rounds. Fire light in a dark room; keyboard only.
  • Minesweeper. The first click is safe; mobile uses long-press to flag.
  • Simon. Has a colorblind mode.
  • 2048. Merges tiles; reach 2048 and chase a new high score.
  • Typewreck. Turns pasted text into a deterministic destructible wall.
  • Saltline. Hold to charge, release to push back the sea.

Six apps

  • Day Pad. A UTC-day countdown plus one saveable line; the line stays in the browser.
  • Timer
  • Tally
  • Dice
  • Coin Flip
  • Convert

Why it feels different

No install

An open link is the whole setup.

Everything runs in a modern browser. There is nothing to download.

No account

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no sign-up, no ads. Free, with no paywall in front of the games.

Your device

Progress stays yours.

No cookies, trackers, or PII. Progress and play counts stay in your browser's localStorage.
