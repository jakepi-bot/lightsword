# Voice sheet — flambeee

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Due:** 2026-11-14 · **Owner:** Content Lead
**Source:** `plans/client-brief-flambeee.md` §4 and the client's own public
writing (flambeee.com, public README, BLOG, release notes). No internal
flambeee file was read.

## 1. The voice in one line

Direct, technically precise, dry, and exact. The same person who wrote the
release notes also writes the marketing; the marketing is allowed to be longer,
not louder.

## 2. Rules

1. **Short sentences. Specific over adjectives.** Name the thing, the number,
   and the limitation.
2. **Never overclaim. Exact numbers only.** If a number is not measured, do not
   use it. Use the measured number and its unit; no rounding up, no "hundreds",
   no implied precision. State the limitation as plainly as the claim.
3. **Plain punctuation. No em dashes.** Periods, commas, colons.
4. **"flambeee" lowercase** for the wordmark and product. "Flambeee" only when
   the company is the grammatical subject.
5. **At most one emoji, and only where it genuinely fits.** Usually zero.
6. **No hype vocabulary.** See the avoid list. If a sentence works without the
   adjective, delete the adjective.
7. **Release posts:** one short paragraph, 300 graphemes max, and include
   `https://flambeee.com`.
8. **Marketing posts:** may be longer, but must sound like the same person.
   Same nouns, same restraint, same exactness.
9. **No hashtags, no engagement bait, no "thought leadership".**
10. **The link is the point.** Send the reader to the specific game or app,
    never to a generic homepage, unless the post is about the company.

## 3. Use / avoid

**Use:** play, browser, free, no account, no install, no ads, no tracking, on
your device, small, sharp, pick one and go, daily, streak, memory, paste,
wreck, quest, UTC day, release, fix, version.

**Avoid:** revolutionary, immersive, epic, world-class, seamless, leverage,
synergy, best-in-class, addictive, viral, soon, coming soon, AI-powered, free
forever, unlimited, secure, anonymous, GDPR compliant, WCAG compliant,
community-driven, players love it, "we're proud to", "we are excited".

## 4. Worked examples

Five worked examples: two good posts, two bad posts with the fix, one release
post. The shape at the end is a template, not a sixth example.

### Good — game post

> Afterglow is a memory maze played in the dark. No install, no account. Try it: https://flambeee.com/games/afterglow.html

Why it works: one concrete mechanic, then the two friction facts, then the
specific link. 120 characters including the URL.

### Good — app post

> Day Pad shows today's UTC date, the time left to midnight UTC, and one line you can save. The line stays in your browser: https://flambeee.com/apps/index.html

Why it works: three facts about the app, the privacy note stated as a benefit,
and a specific link. 158 characters including the URL.

### Bad — then the fix

> 🔥 Get ready to be blown away by our revolutionary new arcade! 🚀

Fix:

> Eight free browser games. Pick one and play. No account, no install.

Why the first fails: hype, emoji stacking, no fact, and it asks the reader to
feel something instead of telling them what the thing is.

### Bad — then the fix

> Flambeee is the best place for seamless, addictive gaming.

Fix:

> Flambeee builds small browser games. No install, no account, no ads. Progress stays on your device.

Why the first fails: "best", "seamless", and "addictive" are unsourced and
against the voice. The fix names the category and the three friction facts.

### Release post

> v0.47.0: Afterglow's play count now clears the fold on a small phone. At 375x667 the line sits at top 625, bottom 640 against a 667px viewport: zero scroll, zero horizontal overflow, 79 pixels higher than v0.46.0. https://flambeee.com

Why it works: the number and the viewport are the announcement. No adjective
is doing the work. 234 characters, inside the 300-grapheme limit.

### Release-post shape

> v0.47.0: [one line on what changed, with the number or the device]. [one
> line on what did not change, if that is the honest note]. https://flambeee.com

The bracket is filled from the release body, never from memory. If the release
body does not support a line, leave the line out.
