## 3. Audience and the one message

- **Primary:** casual mobile break players. Two minutes, thumb-sized screen, no patience for friction.
- **Pain:** every other free game wants an install, signup, ad, or cookie banner before the first move.
- **Secondary:** daily word/puzzle players (Wordfire, Cinder, Afterglow).
- **Not the audience:** esports/competitive, multiplayer, leaderboards, console/AAA, retro/BBS, developers, under-13.
- **One message, every post:** "Pick a game and play in your browser. Nothing to install, no account, no ads." (client-approved)

## 4. Voice

**Direct, dry, precise, exact.** The release-note writer, allowed to be longer, never louder.

**Never in public:** (1) a number we did not measure: no player counts, plays, traffic, retention, "growing", "viral"; (2) anything unshipped: no roadmap, teaser, "soon", future feature; technical internals stay on GitHub; (3) hype or voice breaks: no revolutionary/immersive/epic/seamless/addictive/"free forever", no hashtags (zero, not five), no em dashes, never a Lightsword-voice sentence.
